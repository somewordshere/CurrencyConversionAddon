Twinprice

Two steps and you are done.

Twinprice reads prices on shopping pages inside your browser and shows what they cost in your own money, beside the original.

1

Which currency do you think in?

Prices will be converted into this one. You can change it any time from the toolbar popup.

2

Switch on the tabs you already have open

Twinprice loads itself into pages as they open, so tabs you opened before installing it are not being watched yet. This turns them on without a reload.

Why the install asked for access to every site

A price can be on any shop, so the detector has to be allowed to look at any page. That permission is what lets Twinprice notice a price without you clicking anything first.

  • Reading is all it does. Detection, conversion and drawing happen on your machine.
  • No browsing data is sent. Not page text, not prices, not the addresses you visit.
  • One thing leaves your browser: a three-letter code such as USD, to fetch that day's reference rate.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking. The source is public under the MIT licence.

Priced something wrong, or missed a shop? Get help on the Twinprice website.
